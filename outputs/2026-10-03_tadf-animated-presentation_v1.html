10

Adopt the device, not the headline

TADF offers a route to useful singlets. Adoption needs three tests.

1Lower electrical power at matched colour and output
2Measured operating life that meets the customer’s target
3Repeatable manufacture at acceptable cost

Better physics becomes a better product when all three hold.

Conditional conclusion from the physics, device measurements and bounded commercial evidence
