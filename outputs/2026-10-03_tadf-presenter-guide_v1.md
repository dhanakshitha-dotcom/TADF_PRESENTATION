# Presenting “A better blue pixel”

Open `2026-10-03_tadf-animated-presentation_v1.html` in Chrome or Edge. Everything needed by the slides, models and product image is inside that file; no server, installation or internet connection is needed. The external reference links need internet if opened.

- **F**: fullscreen. **Right / Space**: next slide; on slide 5, the first press reveals the emission trade-off. **Left**: previous slide.
- **P**: pause/resume motion. **R** on slide 4: replay the electrical pulse.
- **N**: show speaker notes. These are visible in the same window, so hide them before projecting. Use the separate script on a second device for private notes.
- **Home**: opening. **End**: closing slide. **B**: first model backup.
- The bottom toolbar appears more clearly on hover or keyboard focus. Simulations have labelled buttons and sliders.

## Presentation sequence

11 spoken slides, targeting 13:00 content plus about 30 seconds across transitions. Four further slides hold model equations, parameters, lifetime/cost context and source links. The supplied timing is a rehearsal budget, not a measurement of your speaking speed.

For slide 4, click Replay pulse and narrate the prompt and delayed parts. The playback lasts 18 seconds and maps logarithmic physical time from 0.1 ns to 1 ms; the full discussion has 55 seconds allocated. Population markers illustrate a deterministic ensemble. For slide 5, explain exchange, reveal emission, then use Weak coupling → Strong coupling → Low overlap → Compromise. For slide 8, use Low G → High G, then move to the separate experimental panel.

## Static fallback

The PDF contains the same 15 slides with motion removed. The pulse is at its final state, the design chart uses the compromise preset, and the density chart shows both RISC cases at high generation. Talk through the plotted curves rather than clicking. It is intentionally static and cannot reproduce every slider state.

## Scientific boundaries

The simulations use illustrative rate constants; they are not fits to TMCz-BO, manufacturer devices or a phone. The reference without RISC is singlet-only fluorescence. The overlap parameter is not an actual molecular angle. Net quadratic triplet loss is not synonymous with every TTA event. The experimental panel re-plots two Table 2 values from Kim et al. (2020), without claiming a measured full curve or a lifetime result. Commercial claims retain their colour and evidence-stage qualifiers.
