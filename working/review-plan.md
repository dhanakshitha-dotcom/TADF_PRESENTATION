# Independent review of the TADF presentation plan

Reviewed 1 October 2026 against the draft, project filing instructions, and the lead agent's confirmed assignment/chat context. This is a content and physics review, not verification of a finished slide deck or independent re-reading of every chat.

**Verdict:** Suitable for user review after the small figure-language correction below. No substantive physics error found in the displayed equations when read with their stated assumptions. Do not create the deck before the user's review.

## Required before building the slides

- Remove the tentative Fig. 5c pointer until the original panel and axes have been checked. An EQE-versus-luminance curve demonstrates operating-brightness efficiency; it does not independently establish TTA as the dominant loss mechanism, operational lifetime, or degradation chemistry. Keep this distinction explicit in slide 9 and its script. The lead agent has confirmed this correction will be made.
- Preserve the main-slide qualifiers when turning this detailed plan into slides: uncorrelated spin statistics for 25:75; spin-pure electric-dipole approximation for the zero matrix element; common-orbital single-configuration approximation for twice the exchange integral; schematic effective coupling for the RISC expression; and steady-state first-order regime for triplet density. These are already present in the plan and must not disappear in presentation shorthand.
- Keep proprietary chemistry unassigned. The phone is a target application, the WiseChip examples are commercial yellow/PMOLED precedents, and LG is a competing company-reported hybrid phosphorescent result. None establishes a commercial blue TADF phone.

## Coverage and timing

- The plan includes a coherent 13-slide walkthrough, every proposed main and backup equation, specific product examples, and explicit claim boundaries. This meets the user's request for a reviewable plan rather than a deck.
- Timings add correctly to 810 seconds (13:30), within the confirmed 12–15-minute brief. Scientific content occupies most of the talk, consistent with the user's emphasis on quantum physics and the design trade-off.
- Slides 5 and 6 are the conceptual centre. Their 80-second allocations are plausible only with staged diagrams and concise spoken definitions; defer the exchange integral and full rate equation to questions as proposed.
- Three commercial entries in 75 seconds is ambitious but workable as a compact comparison. Avoid adding detailed product specifications or turning this section into an advertising segment.

## Equation and evidence checks

- Photon energies, spin-state counting, selection-rule factorisation, exchange splitting, radiative-rate scaling, Förster transfer, illustrative EQE calculations, yield-cost arithmetic, and lifetime endpoint definitions are internally consistent.
- The plan appropriately distinguishes a small gap from sufficient spin-changing coupling and avoids an artificial threshold at kBT.
- The EQE bookkeeping warning is important: define harvesting and effective radiative yield without counting ISC/RISC losses twice. The 5%/20% example remains an illustration, not a universal efficiency bound.
- The backup TTA equation explicitly states a two-triplet-loss toy convention and notes branching/recycling limitations; retain that note if the equation is used in questions.
- Whole-phone carbon data, a metal-free emitter, panel efficiency, battery life, and manufacturing profitability are kept as separate claims. This is a strength of the plan.

## Optional refinement

In slide 13's acceptance criteria, say "electrical power at matched colour and light output" to make the comparison basis immediate. No additional equation or research example is needed for the review-stage plan.
