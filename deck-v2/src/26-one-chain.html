01Voltage creates electrons and holes.
02Electrons and holes create excitons.
03Exciton spin creates singlets and triplets.
04The spin selection rule: singlets emit easily, triplets don’t.
05TADF makes the singlet–triplet gap small.
06Thermal energy plus spin-state coupling drives RISC.
07RISC converts triplets back into singlets.
08And the singlets produce photons.

Everything else in TADF OLED physics is about making each step faster, more efficient and more stable.
