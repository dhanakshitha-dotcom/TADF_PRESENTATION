An OLED is a sandwich of molecules

Voltage in. Charges move. Light out.

Anodesupplies holes (positive)
Cathodesupplies electrons
Organic layerstransport the charges until they meet

Organic semiconductors are built from individual molecules, not a continuous crystal. So we think in molecular energy levels.
