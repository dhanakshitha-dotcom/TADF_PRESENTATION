Two levels matter: HOMO and LUMO

LUMOlowest unoccupied molecular orbital

The molecular version of the conduction band. An injected electron sits here.

HOMOhighest occupied molecular orbital

The molecular version of the valence band. A hole is an empty state here.

Silicon: bands in a crystal. Organics: discrete levels on each molecule.
